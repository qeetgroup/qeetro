# Qeetro Strategy

This directory is the strategic source-of-truth for Qeetro. Every product, engineering, design, GTM, and hiring decision must be traceable back to the principles encoded here.

These documents are not marketing collateral. They are operating documents. They are owned by the founder and the strategy function, and they evolve continuously — but never casually.

## How to use these documents

- **Before building a feature:** read [Product Principles](05-product-principles.md) and [Positioning](03-positioning.md). If the feature does not strengthen one of the moats in [07](07-differentiation-moats.md), it is suspect.
- **Before launching a campaign:** read [Category Design](04-category-design.md) and [ICP](06-icp.md).
- **Before hiring:** read [Manifesto](00-manifesto.md) and [Vision](01-vision.md).
- **Before saying yes to anything:** read [Product Principles](05-product-principles.md) — specifically the "What Qeetro Will Never Be" section.

## Versioning

These documents are versioned by git history. Material strategic shifts must be proposed via PR with a written rationale. The founder is the final approver on Vision, Mission, Positioning, and Category Design. Other documents can evolve faster but must remain internally consistent.
