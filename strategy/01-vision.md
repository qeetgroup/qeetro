# Vision

> **In ten years, the way software organizations execute will look nothing like it does today — and Qeetro will be the substrate that made the transition possible.**

## The 10-year worldview

By 2036, the work-management category as it exists today will be obsolete. The category will have bifurcated into two surviving forms:

1. **Legacy ITSM/PPM systems** — Jira, ServiceNow, and their descendants — confined to compliance-heavy enterprise IT functions. Shrinking, defensive, slow.
2. **AI-native execution systems** — a new category, of which Qeetro is the defining instance — used by every organization where software is the product or the dominant cost center.

The transition will be driven by four irreversible shifts:

### Shift 1: AI agents become first-class organizational members

By 2030, every product organization will have AI agents that own backlog grooming, sprint composition, dependency resolution, status synthesis, risk detection, and even narrow execution tasks (writing tests, drafting PRs, updating docs). These agents need a substrate that treats them as peers — with identity, permissions, accountability, and audit trails. Today's PM tools cannot represent agents at all.

### Shift 2: The end of self-reported state

The single largest waste in modern software organizations is humans reporting state that the system already knows. Standups, status reports, weekly updates, executive dashboards — all of these exist because the substrate cannot infer state from the underlying signals (commits, PRs, deploys, conversations, designs, customer signals). In ten years, self-reported status will look as antiquated as manually compiling code.

### Shift 3: Coordination as the dominant cost

As AI eliminates more individual-contributor work, the residual cost of software organizations becomes coordination — alignment, prioritization, dependency management, cross-team handoffs, decision propagation. Whichever substrate drives coordination cost toward zero wins the decade.

### Shift 4: The collapse of the toolchain

The current "toolchain" model — where teams stitch together Jira + Slack + Notion + GitHub + Figma + Linear + Productboard + Loom — is unsustainable. Context fragmentation is the silent killer of velocity. The winning model is a single execution substrate with deep integrations at the edges, not a federation of equals.

## The Qeetro 10-year vision

Qeetro will be the **default execution substrate for AI-native software organizations** — the system that:

- **Knows the state of the work** without humans having to report it.
- **Hosts both human and AI collaborators** as first-class participants with consistent identity, permissions, and accountability.
- **Drives coordination cost toward zero** through intelligent routing, proactive surfacing, and automated synthesis.
- **Replaces the meeting** as the primary coordination mechanism for distributed teams.
- **Powers a marketplace** of workflow agents, integrations, and execution intelligence built by a global ecosystem.
- **Operates at the scale** of the largest software organizations on earth — 50,000+ engineers, millions of artifacts, sub-100ms responsiveness — without losing the speed and clarity that made it adoptable at the 10-person tier.

## The horizon-by-horizon vision

### Horizon 1 (Years 0–2): Earn the right to exist

Qeetro becomes the obvious choice for ambitious, AI-native software teams of 10–500 people. We win on:

- Onboarding speed (under 5 minutes to first value)
- Core interaction speed (sub-100ms everywhere)
- Native AI that demonstrably reduces coordination work
- Developer experience (API, CLI, SDK, webhooks, deep Git integration)
- Realtime collaboration as a primitive

In this horizon, Qeetro is positioned against Linear and GitHub Projects in the bottom-up motion, and as a "modern Jira replacement" in mid-market deals.

### Horizon 2 (Years 2–5): Define the category

Qeetro becomes the canonical example of "AI-native execution system." We:

- Establish the category language and own the analyst conversation.
- Launch the **Qeetro Agents** platform — a marketplace where third parties build AI agents that operate inside customer workspaces with scoped permissions.
- Win the first wave of large enterprise deals (5,000+ seats) on the strength of governance, AI safety, and operational scale.
- Move upmarket without compromising the bottom-up DNA.
- Make "we use Qeetro" a recruiting signal for top engineering talent.

### Horizon 3 (Years 5–10): Become the substrate

Qeetro becomes infrastructure. We:

- Are the system of record for software execution at a meaningful share of the Global 2000.
- Power workflows that span engineering, product, design, support, sales engineering, and revenue operations.
- Host an agent ecosystem with thousands of third-party agents and tens of thousands of integrations.
- Define the API standards that the rest of the industry builds against.
- Are referenced, in retrospect, as the company that ended the era of configuration-driven work management.

## What success looks like

A founder, in 2034, starting a new company, opens their laptop on day one and creates a Qeetro workspace before they create a GitHub organization. Their AI agents are configured before their first hire. Their first product spec is drafted in collaboration with a Qeetro agent that already knows their market, their constraints, and their goals. They never seriously consider an alternative — not because Qeetro has locked them in, but because the alternatives have become unthinkable.

That is the future we are building toward.

## What we refuse to be in this future

- We refuse to be a tool that humans serve. The substrate serves the humans.
- We refuse to be a surveillance layer. We measure outcomes, not keystrokes.
- We refuse to be a walled garden. Data is portable. APIs are open. Lock-in is earned through quality, not engineered through friction.
- We refuse to be a feature factory. We will ship less than our competitors and win because of it.

The vision is not "a better Jira." The vision is the **end of the category Jira created**, and the beginning of a new one.
